What I’m building

ArcOS — a compound-AI engine

Architect & principal engineer · 2025–present

A five-stage inference pipeline — perception → strategy → assembly → generation → memory — on one principle: perception belongs to the language model, while control, persona, and routing are hard-coded in deterministic logic (75 decision blocks in all). The result holds a consistent persona and durable long-term memory across sessions and across underlying models (Gemma, Llama, Qwen) — swapping the backbone is a config change, not a rewrite.

A 24/7 AI companion app

First production deployment of ArcOS

The first real-world deployment of ArcOS: a 24/7 AI companion for everyday support and reflection — live on the web and on desktop (macOS, Windows, Linux), with a native iOS app in development. It’s the proof that ArcOS holds a stable persona and remembers context over the long term in real use.
